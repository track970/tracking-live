About GlobalSky Airways

Connecting Continents with Safety, Luxury & Precision

GlobalSky Airways operates long-haul passenger flights across key global aviation corridors. Our flagship intercontinental route directly connects West Africa through South Africa’s premier transit hubs into major North American regional airports, including Greater Rochester International Airport (ROC).

Equipped with modern wide-body aircraft, state-of-the-art live flight tracking tools, and comprehensive passenger transit support, we ensure your journey from Accra (ACC) to Rochester (ROC) is comfortable, secure, and fully transparent.
